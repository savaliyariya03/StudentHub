📖 About Us

StudentHub is an online platform designed to make campus life simpler and more organized. It provides students with a single place to access academic information, manage profiles, stay informed about college events, and get updates.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide an easy-to-use digital platform where students can manage their academic activities and stay connected with their institution.

👁️ Our Vision

To create a modern campus environment where technology supports learning, communication, and student development.

🌟 Key Features

  • Student Registration & Secure Login
  • Personal Dashboard & Profile Management
  • Event Information & College Notices
  • Contact, Support & Feedback System
  • Admin Management Panel & FAQ Page
